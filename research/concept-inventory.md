# Concept inventory — *The Quiet Morning* → Hono

Required vocabulary to translate `the_quiet_morning_sentences.csv` (111 sentences)
into a Hono language generated by the `conlang` tool, diffed against the tool's
built-in **175** `(gloss, pos)` defaults (read from
`conlang.sampling.concepts.DEFAULT_CONCEPTS`).

Sources: the story CSV; the tool defaults + `README.md` (`concepts` / `lexicon`
spec keys — both take `gloss` + `pos`, and an explicit `lexicon` is a complete
word list); the Kah precedent `learn_kah/learning-records/0039-...`; the Muna gap
log `muna/translation-gaps.md`.

Usage: the **covered** set can be pinned straight from the defaults. The
**growth list** is what a Hono `lexicon:` block must add (or that `word_count`
must be raised past 175 to sample). POS is the rough part of speech the story
needs; it is a request to the generator, not a claim about Hono's morphology.

| **Clara** | adapted borrowing (Kah used `kalara`) |
| **Gull Island** | calque *island* + *gull* (Kah: `Solan Sokal`, head-first); or loan |
| **the Atlantic** | calque *great sea* / *western ocean* (Kah: `Bamboso`); or loan |

Recommend: coin Hono-legal adaptations for Iain/Clara, calque Gull Island and the
Atlantic from `island`, `gull`, `sea`, `ocean` so they stay transparent.

## 5. Especially hard to translate

Ranked by how much grammar (not vocabulary) they demand.

1. **Aspect and anteriority** — *was rising, was carrying, had been planning*.
   Needs progressive and pluperfect markers; the single biggest structural gap in
   the Muna precedent (LR 0047/0048).
2. **Abstract mental nouns** — *mind, mood, reality, presence, understanding,
   permission, grief, exhaustion, urge, knack, presence*. High risk of circular
   definitions; Muna quarantined several.
3. **Comparatives and equatives** — *louder, stronger, as still as, best*. No
   degree morphology in the tool; Muna never fully closed this.
4. **Non-finite clauses** — *taking in the breeze, looking back, nesting on the
   hill, weathered by salt, oiled lock, cloth-wrapped object, dew-kissed grass*.
   English participles/gerunds with no direct equivalent; Muna recast them as
   juxtaposed or temporal clauses.
5. **Voice and passive-like constructions** — *was weathered by decades, were
   stacked*. No passive in the tool's syntax; needs recasting to active.
6. **Light-verb idioms** — *take in, let go, sort through, set up, roll up, come
   to, head toward, wind upward*. Each is a lexical unit, not a transparent
   composition.
7. **Relative and subordinate clauses** — *who used to say, where the ruins
   stood, that doesn't argue back, until your weather clears*. Relativizer and
   a full linker inventory required.
8. **Culture / technical nouns** — *clinker-built, blueprint, varnish,
   ammunition, manifesto, ledger, rowlock, oilcloth*. Muna left several as
   quarantined slots; expect calque-or-loan decisions per item.
9. **Specific flora and fauna** — *sea pink, rosemary, thyme, lavender, moss,
   stonechat, gannet, sand eel*. Fine-grained; each needs its own row.
10. **Colour and quality nuance** — *turquoise, brilliant, glassy, weathered,
    motionless, persistent*. Higher than basic-colour resolution; some will have
    to be paraphrased.
11. **Articles, definiteness, and pronouns** — absent from the defaults entirely;
    the tool's morphology may not yet express them and they recur in every
    sentence.
12. **Numerals beyond three, ordinals, partitives** — *six, fifty, half, pair,
    decade, first*. Only `one/two/three` exist.
